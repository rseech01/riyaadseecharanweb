001 / Background
Technology meets public purpose

At the Miami-Dade Innovation Authority, Riyaad serves as Pilot Manager, running technology demonstration pilots that pair startups with county data across transportation, solid waste, and water and sewer infrastructure — the unglamorous civic plumbing that shapes daily life for millions of residents.

As a Baptist Health Knight Innovation Fellow, he works at the seam of health, technology, and community. He continues to serve as CTO of ELMC Rx Solutions, the healthcare company that acquired Tesser Health, and operates the Seecharan Group as his advisory and venture practice.

Rooted in the Caribbean with family ties to Trinidad and Tobago, he lives and builds in Miami.

002 / The Arc
From NASA to civic tech
1996 – 2000 · NASA Kennedy Space Center

Knowledge Management & Innovation

Inside the Center for Innovation and Knowledge Management, Riyaad contributed to early work on capturing and connecting institutional knowledge across large organizations. He worked on systems including SAGE and Expert Seeker — both built to help people locate subject-matter experts across distributed networks — as well as intranet prototypes for the International Space Station program. The throughline of that work — using software to connect people to information they couldn't otherwise reach — has quietly run through everything he's built since.

2000 – 2006 · EisenWorld → Microsoft

Commercial Software & Acquisition

As Director of Product Development at EisenWorld, Riyaad led the team behind the PC migration tool that eventually shipped inside Windows 7 — work that culminated in the company's acquisition by Microsoft.

2014 – Present · Healthcare Technology

Building, Scaling & Exiting

Healthcare became the focus in 2014 when he founded Tesser Health to apply behavioral science and technology to pharmacy and medical cost reduction for self-insured employers; the company was later acquired by ELMC Rx. He went on to serve as Chief Product Officer at EyeCheq, helping raise roughly $8M in Series A funding and reach a $40M valuation. Along the way he built and sold several other ventures: PxSource was acquired by RevEHR, while ClinicCover, GetPlano, and EHR were acquired by Barti Software. Across these ventures his work has covered HIPAA-compliant platforms, SaaS architecture, patient engagement, and product strategy.

Now · Civic Innovation

Miami-Dade Innovation Authority

A serial healthcare technology entrepreneur turned public innovator. At MDIA, Riyaad manages technology demonstration pilots connecting startups with county data — bridging the gap between the startup ecosystem and the infrastructure systems that serve millions of Miami-Dade residents daily.
